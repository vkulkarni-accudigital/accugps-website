05 / Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

How is this different from the OBD II tracker?

The LTE tracker is hardwired to your vehicle's power system. It works with any vehicle, not just those with an OBD II port, and allows more flexible placement inside the vehicle.

What happens after the first year?

Ongoing tracking requires a software subscription. Renewal options are $12.99/month or $129/year per device, your choice.

Is professional installation required?

The device connects to your vehicle's 12V power supply. Some customers install it themselves; others prefer a professional. Contact us for guidance on your vehicle type.

What if I have a mixed fleet?

AccuGPS trackers work across different vehicle types from one platform. Contact us to review the right tracker for each vehicle in your fleet.

Where do I check returns and warranty terms?

Contact customer care for current returns and warranty terms before ordering.
