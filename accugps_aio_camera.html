05 / Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

Do I still need the MDVR system?

No. The All-In-One Camera captures road and driver views in one device. The MDVR system is for fleets that need up to 4 camera views.

What does the subscription cover?

The $27/month per vehicle subscription covers cloud storage, live tracking, and AI insights, billed separately from the device.

How does it mount?

The camera mounts to the windshield and auto-starts with the vehicle's ignition.

Will it work with my vehicle?

It's built for commercial vehicles running 8V to 36V DC power. Send us your vehicle's make, model, and year if you're not sure it fits.

Where do I check returns and warranty terms?

Contact customer care for current returns and warranty terms before ordering.
