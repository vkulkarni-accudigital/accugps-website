05 / Before you choose

A few things
worth knowing.

What happens after the first year?

Ongoing tracking requires a software subscription. Renewal options are $12.99/month or $129/year per device, your choice.

Will it work with my vehicle?

The tracker uses a compatible OBD II port. Send us your vehicle’s make, model, and year so we can help you confirm the fit.

Do I need a professional installation?

The OBD II device plugs into the vehicle’s port without hardwiring. Contact us if you need help locating the port or planning your installation.

What if my fleet needs a different tracker?

AccuGPS also offers 4G LTE and magnetic asset trackers. The right option depends on what you track and how you want to install it.

Where do I check returns and warranty terms?

Review the current terms on the AccuGPS store listing before ordering. Contact customer care if you need clarification.
